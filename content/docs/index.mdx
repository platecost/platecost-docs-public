## What is PlateCost?

PlateCost is an **AI-powered food cost intelligence platform** that transforms how restaurants and distributors manage food costs. Upload an invoice and get instant visibility into spending, pricing trends, and recipe costs.

- **Automated invoice processing** — AI extracts line items, prices, and quantities from any invoice format
- **Real-time cost tracking** — See exactly what you're spending and where costs are changing
- **Recipe costing** — Calculate plate costs with live pricing from your actual invoices
- **Vendor comparison** — Compare prices across distributors to find the best deals
- **Works with any vendor** — US Foods, Sysco, Gordon Food Service, Ben E. Keith, and more

<Tip>
No POS integration required. PlateCost works entirely from your invoices — upload a photo, PDF, or email and the AI handles the rest.
</Tip>
