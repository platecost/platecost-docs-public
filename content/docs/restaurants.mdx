PlateCost gives restaurants complete visibility into food costs — from invoice to plate — without requiring a POS integration or changing your workflow.

## The problem

Most restaurants don't know their true food cost until it's too late. Invoices pile up, prices change without notice, and recipe costs drift upward silently. By the time you see it in your P&L, the damage is done.

## Key features

### Automated invoice processing

Upload invoices in any format — PDF, photo, email — and PlateCost's AI extracts every line item automatically. No manual data entry. No spreadsheets.

Supported vendors include:
- US Foods
- Sysco
- Gordon Food Service
- Ben E. Keith
- Performance Food Group
- Any regional or local distributor

### Real-time cost tracking

Your dashboard updates in real time as new invoices are processed. Track:

- **Category spend** — proteins, produce, dairy, dry goods, beverages, supplies
- **Item-level trends** — see how individual ingredient prices change week over week
- **Vendor-level analysis** — understand your total spend and pricing by distributor

### Recipe and plate costing

Build your menu in PlateCost and link ingredients to invoice line items. As prices change, your recipe costs update automatically.

- Create recipes with exact quantities and units
- See theoretical vs. actual food cost
- Model menu price changes and their impact on margins

<Tip>
Target food cost for most restaurants is 28-35% of revenue. PlateCost helps you stay in that range by making cost drift visible immediately.
</Tip>
