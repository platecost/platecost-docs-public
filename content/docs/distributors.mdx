## How it works

### For your customers

Restaurants upload invoices from all their vendors (including yours and competitors). PlateCost gives them a unified view of their food costs, trends, and opportunities.

### For your sales team

Your reps get a dashboard showing:

- **Account health** — which customers are actively engaged
- **Competitive pricing data** — what customers are paying competitors for the same items
- **Upsell opportunities** — categories where you have competitive pricing but low share
- **Churn risk signals** — declining order volume or increasing competitor share

### For your organization

- **Differentiated value proposition** — offer something your competitors don't
- **Data-driven sales conversations** — arm reps with pricing intelligence
- **Customer success at scale** — automate the cost analysis that used to require manual work

## White-label deployment

PlateCost deploys under your brand with:

- Your company logo and colors
- Your custom domain (e.g., `costs.yourdistributor.com`)
- Your support contact information
- Co-branded communications to your customers
