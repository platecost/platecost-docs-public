---
title: Recipe Costing
description: Calculate plate costs with real-time pricing from your actual invoices
---

PlateCost's recipe costing connects your menu items to your actual invoice data — so plate costs update automatically as ingredient prices change.

## Creating a recipe

<Steps>
  <Step title="Navigate to Recipes">
    Go to **Recipes > New Recipe** in your dashboard.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Add ingredients">
    Search for ingredients by name. PlateCost matches them to items from your invoices, pulling in the latest pricing automatically.

    For each ingredient, specify:
    - **Quantity** — how much you use per serving
    - **Unit** — oz, lb, each, cup, etc.
    - **Preparation** — raw, trimmed, cooked (affects yield calculations)
  </Step>

  <Step title="Set yield and portions">
    Define:
    - **Recipe yield** — total output (e.g., 4 quarts of sauce)
    - **Portion size** — how much goes on each plate (e.g., 4 oz per serving)
    - **Number of servings** — auto-calculated from yield and portion size
  </Step>

  <Step title="Review plate cost">
    PlateCost calculates your cost per serving based on current invoice prices. You'll see:
    - **Total recipe cost**
    - **Cost per serving**
    - **Food cost percentage** (at your menu price)
    - **Ingredient cost breakdown**
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Live pricing

Recipe costs are always based on the most recent invoice price for each ingredient. When a new invoice is processed:

- Ingredient prices update automatically
- Recipe costs recalculate
- You can see price change impact on the recipe detail page

<Tip>
Set up price alerts on key recipe ingredients to get notified when costs change significantly. Go to **Settings > Alerts** to configure thresholds.
</Tip>

## Food cost percentage

PlateCost shows food cost percentage for every recipe:

```
Food Cost % = (Cost per Serving / Menu Price) × 100
```

| Food Cost % | Status |
|---|---|
| Under 28% | Excellent margin |
| 28–32% | Healthy range for most concepts |
| 32–35% | Watch closely |
| Over 35% | Action needed — review pricing or portions |

## Menu modeling

Use the **Menu Modeler** to simulate changes before committing:

- **Adjust menu prices** — see how a $1 increase affects food cost %
- **Swap ingredients** — compare costs with alternative products
- **Change portions** — model the impact of portion adjustments
- **Seasonal planning** — project costs based on historical price trends

## Sub-recipes

PlateCost supports nested recipes for complex preparations:

- Create base recipes (sauces, stocks, marinades)
- Reference sub-recipes as ingredients in other recipes
- Costs cascade — when a sub-recipe's ingredients change in price, all parent recipes update

<Info>
Sub-recipes are particularly useful for prep items that appear across multiple menu items. Change the recipe once, and costs update everywhere.
</Info>
